My mission

I believe in always improving — whether it's code or the teams I work with. Inspired by the "Boy Scout Rule" from Robert C. Martin, I aim to leave every project better than I found it.

When I join a project, I want to leave the team in better shape than I found it.

What I do

I've been building software professionally for 25 years, with a focus on code quality throughout. I've been teaching technical subjects since 2004, starting with a Linux programming class while working as a consultant. Over the years, I've led internal courses for companies alongside my consulting work.

In 2017, I decided to fully focus on what I enjoy most: teaching and mentoring. Programming remains at the core of what I do, but I now split my time between writing code and helping others grow through mentoring and training. Services →

Speaking

Since 2011, I've had the privilege of speaking at conferences and user group events around the world. My talks are typically technical, but I always aim to make them engaging and accessible. Sharing knowledge is about more than explaining concepts — it's about inspiring others to think differently and approach problems with a fresh perspective. Talks and podcasts →
